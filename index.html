Quick troubleshooting

  • Serve this page over HTTPS or open via http://localhost (getUserMedia requires a secure context).
  • If the permission prompt never appears, the site may be blocked—check browser site settings (click the camera icon in the address bar).
  • On macOS / Windows, ensure “camera access” is allowed for your browser in system privacy settings.
  • Close other apps (Zoom/Teams) that might be occupying the camera.
